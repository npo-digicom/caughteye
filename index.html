Frequently asked

Where are my entries stored?
Everything stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and no account.

What happens if I delete the app?
Your entries are removed along with it, so please keep a backup of your device if they matter to you.
