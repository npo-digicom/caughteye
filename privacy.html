Privacy Policy

for the iOS app What Caught Your Eye

We collect nothing

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Your entries stay on your device

Every photo and every word you write is stored only on your iPhone. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We have no way to see what you write.

Camera and Photos

The app asks for camera access only so you can take a photo for an entry, and for permission to save that photo to your own photo library. These permissions are used for nothing else.

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no external services.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
